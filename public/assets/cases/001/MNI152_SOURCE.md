# MNI152 T1 atlas

`mni152_t1_2mm.nii` is the decompressed `mni152.nii.gz` sample from
[`neurolabusc/niivue-images`](https://github.com/neurolabusc/niivue-images).

The image is derived from the ICBM 152 Nonlinear 2009 atlas from the
McConnell Brain Imaging Centre. The source repository is distributed under
the BSD 2-Clause license and documents the provenance of this atlas.

This asset is an anatomical visualization reference and is not intended for
clinical diagnosis.
